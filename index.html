ABOUT THE MONTH

What is Cybersecurity Awareness Month?

A month dedicated to building safer digital habits.

🌐

A Global Awareness Campaign

Cybersecurity Awareness Month takes place every October. It encourages individuals, organizations, and communities to learn simple ways to protect themselves in the digital world.

OCTOBER Cybersecurity Awareness Month
🔐

Your Information Matters

Passwords, photos, accounts, messages and personal information all need protection.

💡

Small Actions Matter

Strong passwords, updates, multi-factor authentication and careful clicking can make a real difference.

CYBER SAFETY TOPICS

Know What You Need to Protect

Learn the most important habits for staying safe online.

01
🔑

Strong Passwords

Use long, unique passwords and avoid using the same password across different accounts.

02
🎣

Phishing & Scams

Be careful with urgent messages, unexpected requests, suspicious attachments and fake websites.

03
🔗

Fake Links

Check the website address carefully before entering passwords or personal information.

04
📱

Account Protection

Enable multi-factor authentication and never share verification codes with anyone.

05
🕵️

Privacy

Think carefully before sharing photos, personal details or sensitive information online.

06
💻

Device Security

Keep your operating system and applications updated and avoid suspicious downloads.

07
🧠

Social Engineering

Attackers may manipulate people using fear, urgency or trust to obtain information.

08
🤝

Cyberbullying

Treat others respectfully online and know how to block, report and preserve evidence when necessary.

SIMPLE PROTECTION

Protect Your Digital Life

A few simple habits can strengthen your everyday cybersecurity.

✓

Use unique passwords

Avoid reusing the same password for multiple accounts.

✓

Turn on MFA

Add another verification step to important accounts.

✓

Install updates

Updates can fix security weaknesses in software.

✓

Protect verification codes

Never give one-time codes to someone who asks for them.
